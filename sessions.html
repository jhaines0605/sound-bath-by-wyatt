Before You Arrive

What to expect

Come as you are. There's nothing to prepare and nothing to get right.

  1. Arrive & settle in. Wear something comfortable. Find your place on a mat or settle into your hammock — pillows, blankets, and bolsters welcome. Close your eyes and allow yourself to arrive.
  2. Listen. This is the practice. Listen intently as the first tones enter the room and layers of gongs, crystal bowls, Himalayan bowls, and chimes build around you. There's nothing to follow, visualize, or figure out.
  3. Let the sound unfold. Some sounds are soft and delicate. Others are deep enough to feel through your body. Your mind may quiet, wander, or move between the two. There's no "right" experience.
  4. Return to silence. Eventually the final tones fade and the room becomes quiet again. Take a few moments before opening your eyes and returning to the rest of your day.

First time? You're exactly where you need to be. Just get comfortable and listen.
