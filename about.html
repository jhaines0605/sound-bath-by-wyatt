About Wyatt

A space built around listening

Hi, I'm Wyatt. My journey with sound began 19 years ago at The Organic Bed Shop on Vesta Street in Reno. I went to my first sound meditation not really knowing what to expect. There were gongs — lots of gongs. I settled in, closed my eyes, and listened.

At some point, I found myself floating in an incredible sense of bliss. My mind was quiet. The things I'd been carrying seemed to disappear, and for a brief moment, everything in my world felt okay. It didn't last long. But I've never forgotten how it felt.

I left wanting more, wondering how I could learn to create an experience like that. Luckily, Judy Strauss was offering a beginner's gong class, and I signed up. Judy became my gong master, teacher, and mentor. She shared her knowledge freely and generously — nothing felt guarded. She taught me about the instruments, how to play them, and, just as importantly, how to listen.

Judy has since passed, but what she gave me continues. I'm fortunate to still have a wonderful relationship with her family, and I carry enormous gratitude for the knowledge, encouragement, and experience she shared with me.

Nineteen years later, I'm still playing. My collection has grown from gongs to large quartz crystal singing bowls, hand-hammered Himalayan bowls, chimes, and other deeply resonant instruments. I'm still learning, still exploring sound, and still fascinated by what can happen when we stop trying to meditate and simply listen.

That's what I ask of people who come to my sessions. Listen. Notice the sounds rise, move through the room, overlap, disappear, and return. There's nothing you need to believe, visualize, perform, or get right. Just listen.

I can't promise you'll experience exactly what I did on that floor on Vesta Street all those years ago — every experience is different. All I ask is that you come in, get comfortable, and listen. And maybe, somewhere between the first sound and the final silence, you'll find your own moment when everything feels okay.

— Wyatt

Philosophy

The philosophy behind Sound Bath by Wyatt

Sound has been part of my life for 19 years. Over that time, my appreciation for sound has grown into an equally important appreciation for silence.

I believe many of us have forgotten how to simply listen. We’re surrounded by noise, information, responsibilities, and the constant expectation that we should be doing something.

Sound Bath by Wyatt was created to offer something different.

My work is not about fixing people, promising healing, or telling someone what they should experience. It’s about creating an environment where people have permission to stop, listen, rest, and reconnect with themselves.

There is nothing you need to achieve. There is nothing you need to fix. Just listen.

The Instruments

More than background sound

These sessions are built around large-format gongs, quartz crystal singing bowls, Himalayan bowls, chimes, and other resonant instruments — chosen for the way they fill a room and move through the body. You simply settle in and listen.

The sound builds slowly, surrounds you, and gives your attention somewhere effortless to rest. For many people, the mind becomes quieter, the body softens, and an hour passes without having to try to relax.

A moment of sound — tap to listen.
