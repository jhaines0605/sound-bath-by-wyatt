The Experience

What is a sound bath?

A sound bath is a listening meditation. You lie back, close your eyes, and listen as layers of sound from large gongs, crystal singing bowls, Himalayan bowls, chimes, and other resonant instruments fill the space around you. There's no chanting to follow, no visualization, and nothing to get right. Just listen.

How the sound works

Large gongs, crystal singing bowls, Himalayan bowls, chimes, and other resonant instruments create layers of sound that move throughout the room. Some tones are delicate. Others are deep enough that you may feel them as much as hear them. As the sounds change, your attention naturally follows — there's no need to force your mind to become quiet. Listen intently, and let the sound give your attention somewhere to rest.

Deep relaxation

Long, sustained tones give your attention somewhere to rest. As the sound fills the room, many people notice the body beginning to soften and the pace of their thoughts slowing down.

Effortless meditation

You don't need to know how to meditate. There's no mantra, visualization, or technique to master. Close your eyes, breathe normally, and listen.

A quiet reset

An hour without a screen, conversation, or anything being asked of you. Just sound, stillness, and time to step away from the noise of everyday life.
